収集する情報

  • 本アプリはアカウント登録・ログインを必要としません。
  • 気分の記録、習慣の名前や達成記録は、お使いの端末内(ローカルストレージ)にのみ保存されます。外部のサーバーへの送信や、開発者を含む第三者への共有は一切行いません。

本アプリをアンインストールすると、端末内に保存された記録もあわせて削除されます。アプリ内から個別の記録・習慣を削除することもできます。

Yuru Habit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Yuru Habit ("the App") is an iOS app provided by an independent developer. This policy explains how information is handled within the App.

Information We Collect

  • The App does not require any account registration or sign-in.
  • Mood entries, habit names, and completion records are stored only on your device (local storage). Nothing is sent to any server, and nothing is shared with the developer or any third party.
  • The App does not access your location, contacts, photo library, camera, or microphone (except when you choose a photo as a habit icon, which is then stored only on your device).
  • The App does not display advertising and does not use any third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data Deletion

Uninstalling the App deletes all records stored on your device. You can also delete individual habits or entries from within the App.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact scrublystore37@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Significant changes will be announced within the App.
